📖 Why this exists

A thank-you too big for one card.

A greeting card felt too small. A speech felt too short. So we built a whole little universe instead — one page for every teacher who made Aakash feel like a second home. 💙

💡 What our teachers gave us

More than syllabus, always

🧠

Concepts that stick

Diagrams on the board at 8am that are still in our heads at midnight before the exam. That's not teaching, that's magic.

💪

Courage before marks

“Mistake is the first step to marks.” They never let a wrong answer feel like the end of the world — just the start of learning.

🏠

A second home

Doubt sessions that ran over time, corridors full of encouragement, classrooms that felt safe. Aakash is family because of them.

⏰ A day in our Aakash life

The routine we'll actually miss

8:00 AM · First bell

“Good morning, batch!” ☀️

Half-asleep faces, full-energy teachers. Attendance happens, then the real show begins — marker squeaks and minds wake up.

11:30 AM · Peak confusion

“Sir, ek doubt tha…” 🙋

One hand goes up, then ten. Nobody is rushed, nobody is judged. Every doubt gets its diagram.

2:00 PM · The legendary line

“Beta, yeh last topic hai” 📚

It is never the last topic. We know it. They know it. The syllabus finishes anyway — that's the real magic.

5:00 PM · Doubt room

“Ruko, main samjhata hoon” 💡

Class ended an hour ago but the teacher is still there, still explaining, still smiling. This page exists because of moments like these.

5th September · Today

“Happy Teacher's Day!” 🎓

Our turn to give back — with letters, wishes and a website they'll (hopefully) show their families tonight.

Dear teachers — you turned our fear of physics into curiosity, our hate for maths into friendship, and our ordinary school days into stories we'll tell for years. Thank you for every extra minute, every repeated explanation, every “tumse ho jayega”.

— your batch, with full attendance and fuller hearts 💙
